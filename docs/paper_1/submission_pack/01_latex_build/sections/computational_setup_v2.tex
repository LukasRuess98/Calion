All levels are implemented in Python with Pyomo~\cite{pyomo}, instantiated from a single
parameterised codebase through YAML configuration files, so that a fidelity level is a
configuration of one model rather than a separate model. This is what makes the
one-phenomenon-per-step contrasts exact: two levels differ in the terms their configuration
activates and in nothing else, and a level with a phenomenon disabled reproduces the level
below it to the cent. Solves use Gurobi~13.0 with identical random seeds on a 66-core,
180\,GB machine, with \texttt{NonConvex=2} where a quadratic formulation is involved.

Optimality tolerances are tighter than in the original submission, and the reason is a
result in itself. The decomposition terms are small relative to the total cost (the
topology main effect is $961$\,EUR against a $136$\,kEUR baseline), so at the $0.5$\,\%
tolerance the original solves used, both the topology and the interaction term sat inside
solver noise, and the interaction carried the opposite sign to its converged value. All
linear levels are therefore solved to a $0.01$\,\% gap with \texttt{MIPFocus=2}, and every
network of the synthetic factorial to $0.1$\,\% or better, so that each reported term sits
well above the attained gap. Achieved gaps are reported alongside the results rather than
assumed from the tolerance.

Not every level is solved as a dispatch problem, and the distinction is stated wherever the
levels are used. The station-resolved levels $\Lfour$ and $\Lfive$ are evaluated by the
validated forward pressure-study module: their station code post-dates the baseline
worktree, and porting it would risk the byte-identical reproduction of the lower levels on
which the exact decomposition depends. Forward evaluation upper-bounds their regret, so the
null they establish is rigorous rather than assumed. Temperature propagation ($\Ltwo$) is
handled the same way for the degeneracy reason given in
Section~\ref{sec:extended}. The instance count is 135 synthetic networks plus the
Memmingen case, each solved through the four decomposition configurations, together with
the ladder levels solved on Memmingen.
