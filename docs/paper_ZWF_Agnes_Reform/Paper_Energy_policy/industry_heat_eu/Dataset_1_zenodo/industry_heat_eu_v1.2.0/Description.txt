This dataset (v1.2.0) provides final energy consumption and useful energy demand (TWh) by category type and temperature level in 12 industrial subsectors for the EU27 aggregate and each Member State. 

Rows correspond to energy demand category:
- Electricity Other, 
- Electricity Thermal, 
- Steam, 
- Non-electric process heat <100°C, 
- Non-electric process heat 100–400°C, 
- Non-electric process heat 400–1000°C, 
- Non-electric process heat >1000°C.

Columns provide subsectoral final energy consumption and useful energy demand in TWh:
- Primary Steel (TWh)
- Secondary Steel (TWh)
- Chemicals (TWh)
- Cement (TWh)
- Pulp and Paper (TWh)
- Food, Beverages and Tobacco (TWh)
- Transport Equipment (TWh)
- Machinery and Equipment (TWh)
- Textiles and Leather (TWh)
- Wood and Wood Products (TWh)
- Non-ferrous Metals (TWh)
- Ceramics and Glass (TWh)

The dataset is intended to support industrial heat-demand mapping, electrification feasibility studies, process heating technology assessments, and energy-system modelling.
Scope note (v1.2.0): the Chemicals column covers the complete JRC-IDEES chemical
industry (basic chemicals, other chemicals and pharmaceutical products),
excluding energy carriers used as feedstock. Pulp and Paper excludes printing
and media reproduction. Mining and quarrying, construction and non-specified
industry are outside the scope of the 12 sub-sectors reported here.

Process-to-category mapping
---------------------------
The assignment of every JRC-IDEES process row to one of the seven demand categories is
distributed with this dataset as mapping/process_category_mapping.csv, and the script
mapping/build_industryheat_eu.py rebuilds every CSV in this archive from a clean download
of JRC-IDEES-2023 plus that file alone.

Conventions worth noting:
- Electricity that supplies heating or cooling is reported as "Electricity Thermal";
  electricity for mechanical power and lighting as "Electricity Other".
- Non-electric process cooling is allocated below 100 deg C, following Madeddu et al. (2020).
- Steam raised in non-electric boilers is reported as its own category; it falls within the
  100-400 deg C range. Steam curing of precast concrete (40-80 deg C) is the one exception
  and is reported below 100 deg C.
- Where JRC-IDEES reports a process only as an aggregate, the aggregate is assigned to the
  band of its dominant step. Thermal pulping, which covers digestion, black-liquor
  evaporation and the lime kiln, is allocated to 100-400 deg C.
- Drying is allocated by the temperature at which the dryer operates: food and textile
  dryers to 100-400 deg C, timber kilns below 100 deg C.
- Feedstock use of energy carriers is excluded throughout.
